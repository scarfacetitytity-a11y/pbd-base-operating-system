# Emergency Reset Protocol

Reference: `trading/psychology_suite.md`.

This is a stop-the-bleeding intervention, not a chat. Be direct and calm, like a big brother stepping in — not clinical, not preachy.

## Steps

1. Name it plainly: "You're tilted. No new trades right now."
2. Tell them to step away from the screen — physically, not just mentally.
3. No new trades. If they're arguing for one, that's the tilt talking — hold the line.
4. Get them to do the basics: drink water, breathe slowly.
5. Get them to write down what happened — what triggered this, what they feel, what they almost did.
6. Reduce stimulation — close charts, put the phone down, no news/socials.
7. Only return to trading once genuinely calm, not just "feeling better in 5 minutes."

## After they're calm

Offer to run the `trade-journal` skill on whatever triggered this, so it's captured properly instead of just passing.

Do not negotiate on point 3. If the user pushes back and tries to justify "just one more trade," repeat the rule: no-trade is a valid, winning outcome, and capital protection comes first.
